# Heartbeats is a Stretch Workshop, and the Saga lab gets its time

Learners rate the Saga Workshop the most useful lab of a Dev Day, so every
Deck gives it more room and an earlier slot. The Saga section and its lab run
straight after the Quiz, ahead of Child Workflows, Continue-As-New and
Heartbeats, and the lab gets 30 minutes instead of 25.

The time comes from Heartbeats, which becomes a Stretch Workshop in every Deck:
its slides are still taught to everyone, but its Track is offered only to
learners who finish early, on both the Saga lab slide and the Labs slide.
Child Workflows (15 minutes) and Continue-As-New (10 minutes) stay core, so the
Labs block drops from 35 to 25 minutes and the day comes out 5 minutes shorter,
kept as buffer.

Stretch describes live delivery only. The Heartbeats Workshop, its Track and
its README row are unchanged; someone taking the Track on their own sees an
ordinary Workshop.

## Considered Options

- **Child Workflows as the Stretch Workshop** was rejected: it is the only one
  of the three at the Foundations Level, and it teaches composition every later
  pattern uses.
- **Continue-As-New as the Stretch Workshop** was considered; learner feedback
  pointed at Heartbeats instead.
- **A STRETCH Challenge inside the Saga Track** was considered for early
  finishers and not added: the Saga's four Challenges fill the 30 minutes.
- **Marking Heartbeats as Stretch in the README index** was rejected: Stretch is
  about how a Dev Day is run, not about the Workshop.

## Consequences

The Saga section now comes before Child Workflows, so the Child Workflows
segment's mention of the Saga section points back to something already taught.

The Python and TypeScript DevDays repos keep their own order until the same
change is made there.
